Most security failures I come across aren't dramatic. Nobody in a hoodie is typing furiously in a dark room. It's a password on a sticky note. An old account nobody switched off. Someone who clicked the link they *knew* they shouldn't.

That's what this blog is about: the cyber risks hiding in plain sight, and how organizations and everyday people can see them before someone else does.

## Why I'm writing this

I work in cybersecurity, and I've learned that teaching something is the fastest way to find out what you really know, and what you don't. So I'm learning out loud.

I also think security is explained badly. It's either buried in jargon or wrapped in fear. I want to do neither. I want to tell the story, explain what actually happened, and leave you with something practical.

## What you'll find here

- **Real incidents, explained simply:** what went wrong, why it happened, and what it cost.
- **Security for organizations:** vulnerability assessments, access control, governance, and why security is a business decision, not just an IT task.
- **Everyday security habits:** from passwords and MFA to choosing your cookies wisely.
- **Behind the build:** what I learn while building and running this very website.
